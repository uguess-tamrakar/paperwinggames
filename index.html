Giant's Causeway (40 FT)

Antrim Coast, Northern Ireland • First Milestone Badge
📜 Historical & Mythic Lore

According to Gaelic myth, the causeway was built by giant Finn McCool as stepping stones across the sea to Scotland to duel his rival Benandonner.

🌋 Geological Formation

Formed 50–60 million years ago during the Paleocene Epoch when molten basaltic lava cooled and contracted into regular hexagonal pillars.

🦅 Wildlife & Features

Home to fulmars, red-billed choughs, and rare sea spleenwort ferns nesting among the sea-cliff crevices.

To-to-kon-oo-lah • Yosemite, USA 🇺🇸

The sheerest 3,000-foot vertical granite monolith on Earth, looming over Yosemite Valley.

High Sierra Monolith 7,573 FT Summit

El Capitan (Yosemite)

Sierra Nevada, California • Tier IV Highland Pass
📜 Mountaineering History

Revered as "To-to-kon-oo-lah" by the Ahwahneechee people; Alex Honnold achieved its historic first rope-free solo ascent in June 2017.

🌋 Geological Formation

Composed of coarse-grained El Capitan Granite intruded roughly 100 million years ago during the Cretaceous and sheared clean by alpine glaciers.

🦅 Wildlife & Features

Peregrine falcons nest on sheer overhanging granite ledges high above the roaring Merced River and Horsetail Fall.

Sacred Stratovolcano 12,389 FT (3,776 m)

Mount Fuji (Fuji-san)

Honshu Island, Japan • Tier VI Alpine Crest
📜 Cultural & Artistic Lore

Revered as one of Japan's "Three Holy Mountains" for centuries and immortalized in Hokusai's woodblock series "Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji."

🌋 Geological Formation

A symmetrical composite stratovolcano formed across three distinct volcanic generations: Komitake, Ko-Fuji, and snow-capped Shin-Fuji.

🌸 Wildlife & Features

Ringed by the pristine Fuji Five Lakes, Aokigahara forest, and vibrant spring cherry blossom reflections.

The undisputed monarch of alpine pyramid horns with four sheer compass-oriented glacial faces.

The Matterhorn

Zermatt / Breuil-Cervinia • Alpine Pyramid Horn
📜 Mountaineering History

Edward Whymper's dramatic first ascent along the Hörnli Ridge in July 1865 marked the grand finale of the Golden Age of Alpinism.

🌋 Geological Formation

A textbook glacial horn sculpted when four separate cirque glaciers eroded inward against a central African tectonic gneiss peak.

🐐 Wildlife & Features

Alpine ibex, chamois, and edelweiss wildflowers thrive along the lower Zermatt meadows beneath its four towering ridges.

King of Mountains • Karakoram 🇵🇰

Earth's second-highest peak and the ultimate technical pyramid of granite and blue ice.

Karakoram Eight-Thousander 28,251 FT (8,611 m)

K2 — Chhogori

Karakoram Range • Tier XII Crown of the World
📜 Mountaineering History

Surveyed as "K2" by the Great Trigonometrical Survey in 1856; conquered in winter for the very first time by a team of 10 Nepali mountaineers in January 2021.

🌋 Geological Formation

A sheer crystalline pyramid of metamorphic gneiss and granite soaring over 10,000 vertical feet above the Godwin-Austen Glacier.

❄️ Signature Feature

Home to the legendary Abruzzi Spur and the House's Chimney high in the jet-stream Death Zone.

The highest point on planet Earth—crowned by its roaring jet-stream snow plume.

#112 Ultimate Crown Badge 29,032 FT (8,849 m)

Mount Everest (29,032 FT)

Mahalangur Himal, Nepal / Tibet • Badge #112
📜 Historic First Ascent

Known as Sagarmatha ("Goddess of the Sky") and Chomolungma; first summited on May 29, 1953 by Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay Sherpa.

🌊 Ancient Ocean Fossils at the Summit

Its summit Qomolangma limestone was once on the floor of the ancient Tethys Sea over 400 million years ago and still holds fossilized crinoids!

🏔️ Signature Feature

The iconic jet-stream snow banner streams from the summit pyramid above the Khumbu Icefall and colorful prayer flags.
