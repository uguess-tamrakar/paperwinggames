Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 · Paper Wing Games LLC

1. Overview

Paper Wing Games LLC ("Paper Wing Games," "we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes mobile games, including Summit Rangers: Tactical Trail Logic. We are committed to providing a safe, entertaining, and privacy-conscious gaming experience for players of all ages.

2. Information We Collect & Local Device Storage

We do not require user accounts and do not directly collect, store, or sell personal contact information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical street address.

  • Local Game Progress: All puzzle progression, altitude records, unlocked badges, daily streaks, Golden Pinecone balances, and settings preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Resetting Local Data: You may clear or reset your local game progression at any time via the in-game Settings menu or by uninstalling the application.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our games integrate standard platform and monetization services that may process limited device and diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy policies and your consent preferences:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to display optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. Depending on your region and consent settings (managed via Google's User Messaging Platform and Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework), Google may access pseudonymous advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android), coarse IP location, and ad interaction metrics for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play In-App Purchases: All financial transactions for in-app purchases (such as the Explorer Pass or Golden Pinecone bundles) are processed directly by Apple or Google. Paper Wing Games never receives or stores your credit card or payment details.

4. Your Privacy Rights & Ad Choices (GDPR / CCPA / ATT)

  • In-App Consent Management: You can review or update your ad consent preferences at any time inside the game by opening Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Device-Level Controls: On iOS, you can manage tracking permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Summit Rangers complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international child privacy regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently received personal information from a child, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
