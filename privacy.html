Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Paper Wing Games LLC

Paper Wing Games LLC ("Paper Wing Games," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our mobile games—including Summit Rangers: Tactical Trail Logic—or visit paperwinggames.com.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your puzzle progress, unlocked mountain tiers, daily expedition streaks, Golden Pinecones, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal contact information to play.

2. Advertising & Analytics Partners

To keep our games free to play, we work with trusted third-party service providers that may process device identifiers (such as your mobile Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse IP-based location, diagnostic crash logs, and ad interaction metrics:

  • Google AdMob: Serves optional rewarded video ads and banner/interstitial placements. Depending on your region and consent preferences, ads may be contextual (non-personalized) or personalized.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics): Helps us diagnose app crashes, maintain stability, and understand aggregate level completion rates.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play Billing: Processes optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads or unlocking tool bundles). Payment details are handled directly by Apple or Google and are never shared with us.

3. Your Privacy Choices & Consent

When launching our games, you can choose between personalized or contextual (non-personalized) advertising experiences, and you can update your privacy and consent settings at any time inside the in-game Settings → Privacy Preferences menu or through your device's system privacy settings (iOS App Tracking Transparency / Android Advertising ID controls).

4. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

5. Data Deletion & Contact Us

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing app storage immediately deletes your local progress. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to submit a privacy request, please contact us at:
